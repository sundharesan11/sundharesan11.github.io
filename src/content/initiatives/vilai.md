What draws me to sport is the discipline of returning to it. Enthusiasm gets someone started; I’m interested in what helps them keep showing up. I’d like to put some of my own energy into making that possible for young people who want to try.

Vilai is a place for my thoughts about giving more young people that chance. For now, I’m writing them down. In time, I’d like to meet people who see something in this and work out what we could do together.

I don’t have a programme to announce. I have an idea I’d like to follow.
