When I imagine koothu reaching the world, I keep coming back to the person who has to make time to practise it tomorrow. I want the art to travel, and I want pursuing it to be a life someone can afford to choose. That is the part I’d like to help work out.

Kalai is where I’m putting that thought for now. I haven’t built an organisation around it. I’m trying to give an idea enough space to become a conversation, and perhaps, eventually, something people can work on together.

I believe in ideas worth practising. This is one of mine.
